**Tika** can be:

* A Nepalese name for [<u>Tilaka</u>](http://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Tilaka)
* A nickname for Petrika the Albanian variation of [<u>Peter</u>](http://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Peter)
* A title in certain Indian monarchies for a [<u>Crown Prince</u>](http://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Crown_Prince)
* A place in [<u>Abkhazia</u>](http://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Abkhazia)
* A place on Saturn's satellite [<u>Rhea</u>](http://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Rhea), named after the last place
* A name in various Indian languages (ṭīkā) for certain commentaries such as:
* the [<u>subcommentaries</u>](http://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Subcommentaries%2C_Theravada) of the [<u>Theravada</u>](http://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Theravada) tradition.
